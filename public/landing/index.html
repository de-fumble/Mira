How it works

Speak. Review. Export.
No more typing it all out.

01

You speak. Mira types.

Read your notes aloud or just talk. Mira types every word at your cursor, as you say it. Say “comma”, “full stop” or “new paragraph” and it punctuates for you.

02

AI checks every sentence.

One tap and Mira proofreads the whole document. It flags misspelt or misheard words, sentences that don’t make sense, and ones that don’t belong — then gives it a score out of 100.

03

Export and send.

Fix things with a tap, then download a clean Word document or PDF — ready to print, submit or email.
