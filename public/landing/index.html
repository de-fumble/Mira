How it works

Listen. Understand. Speak.
One gentle companion.

01

It listens, so you can read.

Mira captions every voice in the room in real time — in type big enough to read from across the table. Each speaker gets their own color, so you always know who said what.

02

It understands, so you can ask.

Missed something? Just ask. “What did she say about the tablets?” Mira answers in plain words, and keeps a short, simple note of what matters.

03

It speaks, so you’re heard.

When speaking is hard, type a few words — or tap a ready phrase — and Mira says it aloud in a warm, natural voice, at a pace that feels right.
